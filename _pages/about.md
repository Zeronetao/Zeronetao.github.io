## About

I am an M.S. student in Computer Technology at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology, and a member of the [Junwei Zhou Research Group](https://junwzhou.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from the School of Cyberspace Security at Hainan University.

My research connects model development, systems engineering, and real-world applications. I am currently interested in multimodal fine-grained diagnosis, root cause analysis for dynamic microservices, large language model fine-tuning and evaluation, and data intelligence for logistics and education.
